# The-Chocolate-Compass : Navigating Global Sales. Building an interactive dashboard on Excel
### Description
In The Chocolate Compass, I transformed a raw chocolate sales dataset into a strategic tool that guides
 decision-making in a competitive market. By meticulously analyzing sales data across different regions, products, and time periods, I uncovered actionable insights that empower stakeholders to make data-driven decisions.
 
![Screenshot 2025-04-13 015319](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e921f16e-a58a-499a-9c7e-71dd533fcef7)


### Key Objectives
- Optimize Sales Performance: Identify top-performing products, leading sales teams, and key market drivers to boost revenue and improve shipment efficiency.
- Market & Regional Analysis: Evaluate global sales dynamics by comparing revenue and order volumes across countries, revealing high-value markets and opportunities for expansion.
- Trend & Seasonality Discovery: Analyze month-over-month and year-over-year trends to forecast demand, streamline inventory management, and capitalize on seasonal peaks.
  
### Analytical Approach
- Data Preparation & Cleaning: Structured the raw dataset for accuracy, ensuring all key metrics—such as total sales revenue, boxes shipped, and orders—were captured.
- Pivot Table Analysis: Leveraged Microsoft Excel to create pivot tables that calculated critical KPIs, including:
Total Sales Revenue and Total Boxes Shipped,
Average Sales and Boxes per Order,
Top and Lowest-Selling Products by both revenue and quantity,
Salesperson and Regional performance metrics,
Month-over-Month Growth rates using calculated columns,
    Interactive Dashboard Development: Developed an intuitive Excel dashboard featuring dynamic charts, slicers, and conditional formatting to present insights visually and interactively.
### Outcome and Impact
The Chocolate Compass provides a comprehensive, at-a-glance view of global chocolate sales performance. This project not only demonstrates my technical proficiency in data analysis and visualization but also showcases my ability to translate complex data into clear, actionable business insights. The resulting dashboard has proven valuable for strategic planning, enabling targeted marketing initiatives, sales performance optimization, and enhanced inventory management.
